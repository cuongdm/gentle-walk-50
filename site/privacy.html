Good Footing Privacy Policy

Last updated: [date of publication]

Good Footing keeps your health data, workouts and answers on your phone. We don't have accounts, ads or tracking, and we never sell your data. The one thing that leaves your phone is your purchase record, so the app knows which plan you have (see Purchases).

What stays on your phone

Your onboarding answers (the name you chose to give, goals, what's made it hard before, how active you are, anything we should go easy on), your workouts, "This hurts" taps, rest days, reminder time and journey progress are stored only on your phone. The app does not back them up to iCloud.

Your 2-week check results stay on this phone, too. They are compared only with your own earlier checks.

Apple Health

If you connect Apple Health, Good Footing reads your step count to show it in Progress and to suggest a lighter session on busy days. It also saves your workouts (and, for outdoor walks, the route) to Apple Health. Health data is never used for advertising and never leaves your phone through us.

Location

Outdoor walks can use your location while you walk, to measure distance and draw your route. Location is used only during an outdoor walk you start, and the route stays on your phone (and in Apple Health if you allow it). You can choose "Just count my steps" instead.

Motion

With your phone held to your chest, the app can count your sit-to-stands from motion data, and it can count steps on outdoor walks without location. Motion data is processed on your phone and not stored.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your phone. They never mention health details on the lock screen.

Purchases

Subscriptions and one-time purchases are made through Apple. We never see your payment details.

To know which plan you have, Good Footing uses RevenueCat, a service that manages in-app subscriptions. The app sends RevenueCat your purchase history (which plan, when it started and renews, its price and whether it is a free trial) under a random ID created on your phone. With it go the technical details needed to check a purchase: the app and iOS version, your App Store country, language and currency, an identifier your iPhone gives to apps from the same developer, and, as with any internet connection, your IP address.

The app never sends RevenueCat your name, email, Apple Account, health data, workouts, answers or location. We use this record only to unlock Pro, to check purchases against fraud, and to see how many people choose each plan. It is not linked to your identity, not used for advertising and not shared for tracking. RevenueCat's own policy: revenuecat.com/privacy.

Deleting your data

In the app, go to Me → Delete all my data. This removes everything the app stored on your phone. Workouts already saved in Apple Health stay there until you remove them in the Health app.

Your purchase record stays with Apple and RevenueCat, so Restore keeps working on a new phone. To have the RevenueCat record deleted, email us and we will ask RevenueCat to remove it.

Children

Good Footing is made for adults and is not directed to children.
