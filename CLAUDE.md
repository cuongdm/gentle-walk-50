App iPhone/iPad dẫn bài tập nhẹ tại nhà bằng giọng (đi bộ trong nhà, động tác ghế, giãn cơ, thăng bằng) cho phụ nữ Mỹ 58–75 mới tập hoặc quay lại (đổi từ 50–64 ngày 03/10/2026). Tên chốt 07/10/2026: store "Good Footing: Gentle Workouts", khẩu hiệu "Steadier on your feet, at your own pace."; tên làm việc cũ "Gentle Walk 50+". Kế hoạch hiện hành: `docs/plans/2026-09-29-mvp.md`. Việc cần làm: `docs/todo.md`. **Bàn giao đang mở (08/10/2026): `docs/handoff/2026-10-08-steady-program-local.md`** — code chương trình vững chân viết trên cloud, chưa build; phiên Mac đọc file này trước.

## Quy ước dự án (manh-skill — đọc trước khi làm bất cứ gì)
- Step 0 of every task: read `app-context.md` (product truth) and the current plan in `docs/plans/`.
- Deployment target: iOS 18.0 (iPhone + iPad), Xcode 27 / SDK 27 — do not propose APIs newer than iOS 18 (e.g. `alert(item:)`, `reorderable()`, toolbar overflow APIs); flag it if unavoidable. `@State` is a macro in SDK 27: on "used before being initialized" read `swiftui-whats-new-27/references/state-macro.md`, never reorder assignments.
- Repo layout (29/09/2026): `iOS/` holds the whole iOS project, `Android/` the future Android app; the root holds what both share: `app-context.md`, `docs/`, `assets/` (source art, music, video, voice), `tools/` (content, art, music, voice builders and copy lint; they write into `iOS/App/…`), `site/`.
- Project is generated by XcodeGen: edit `iOS/project.yml`, run `xcodegen generate` inside `iOS/`; never commit `iOS/GentleWalk.xcodeproj`. Signing lives in `iOS/Config/Local.xcconfig` (git-ignored).
- App name in user-facing text: always `AppBrand.name` (`iOS/App/Design/AppBrand.swift`), interpolated (`"\(AppBrand.name) Pro"`), never typed out; internal names (bundle ID `com.kmd.gentlewalk`, product IDs, `GentleWalk` target/types/files) never change (`docs/design/doi-ten-app-checklist.md` §7).
- Pure product logic goes in `iOS/Packages/GentleWalkCore` (Foundation only, `swift test`); Apple frameworks stay in the `GentleWalk` app target.
- Owner-facing text (reports, checklists, questions) in Vietnamese; code, comments and commit messages in English.

## Kiến trúc & UI
- SwiftUI first: `@Observable` `@MainActor` models with `Equatable` property types; one `struct: View` per section with narrow inputs; no work in `init` (swiftui-specialist `structure.md`, `dataflow.md`).
- `ForEach` / `List` identity: a stable stored id (`Identifiable`); never indices, `\.self` on structs, or ids minted in `body`; rows unary.
- Navigation: `TabView` (Today, Journey, Progress, Me), one `NavigationStack` per tab + one `navigationDestination(for:)` per type; onboarding, players and paywall via `fullScreenCover(item:)`; `sheet(item:)` for optional data.
- No hard-coded screen widths: never `UIScreen.main`; use layout containers, `ViewThatFits`, text styles. Body text ≥ 17 pt, touch targets ≥ 56 pt, contrast ≥ 4.5:1 (design spec).
- Design tokens only: colours from `Palette` (iOS/App/Design/Tokens.swift, asset colour sets with dark variants), fonts via `.typeRole(_:)` (Dynamic Type), buttons via `.buttonStyle(.primaryAction / .secondaryAction / .dangerAction)`, spacing from `Metrics`. Every new text-on-fill pair goes into `Palette.textPairs` so `DesignTokenTests` checks 4.5:1 in light and dark. Preview gallery: `-ScreenshotMode tokens`.
- Data: SwiftData with `VersionedSchema` + `SchemaMigrationPlan`; never delete an old schema version; `cloudKitDatabase: .none` (health data never goes to iCloud).
- Workout audio is one `AVMutableComposition` played by one `AVPlayer` (voice, bells, music + audio mix ducking). Never play silent audio to keep the app alive (2.5.4).
- Content (exercises, journeys, voice lines, notification phrases) is bundled JSON in `iOS/App/Resources/Content/` (built from `docs/scripts/` by `tools/content/build_content.py`); check it with `ContentValidator`.

## Ngôn ngữ (String Catalog)
- All user-facing text goes through `Localizable.xcstrings` / `InfoPlist.xcstrings`; `LocalizedStringResource` for non-literal text; interpolation, never `+`; format styles, never `dateFormat` literals; distance with `Measurement<UnitLength>`; `bundle: #bundle` inside packages.
- Locales: en-US; every new string is translated in every locale before a task is DONE.
- Copy follows `app-context.md` Tone & copy rules; run `python3 tools/lint/copy_lint.py` (banned words, medical claims, other platform names) → `0 findings`.

## Tuân thủ App Review / Play
- Paywall trio on every purchase screen: Restore control (`StoreService.restore()` → RevenueCat `restorePurchases()`), Terms of Use + Privacy Policy links, billed price the most prominent price; trial terms and auto-renewal visible; "Maybe later" always visible; no countdowns or struck-through prices.
- Entitlement truth (owner 09/10/2026, reverses "StoreKit 2 direct, no RevenueCat"): RevenueCat SDK (`purchases-ios-spm`, pinned exact in `iOS/project.yml`), entitlement `pro` of `CustomerInfo` + `customerInfoStream`; offering `default` with `$rc_annual` · `$rc_monthly` · `$rc_lifetime`. Only `App/Services/Store/RevenueCatBackend.swift` imports RevenueCat, behind the `PurchaseBackend` seam of `StoreService`; the rules live in `GentleWalkCore` (`CustomerRules`); tests use a fake backend and never reach the network. Anonymous app user ID (no `logIn`, no accounts), StoreKit 2 mode, no device-identifier collection, `Purchases.logLevel` `.error` in Release. The public SDK key comes only from `iOS/Config/Local.xcconfig` (`REVENUECAT_PUBLIC_KEY` → Info.plist `RevenueCatPublicKey`), never from code; no key → no store, free plan, paywall "Try again" (the release gate fails). Prices and trial come from the store, never typed: yearly 49.99 USD with a 14-day free trial, monthly 9.99, lifetime 99.99. Products: `com.kmd.gentlewalk.pro.yearly`, `.pro.monthly`, `.pro.lifetime`.
- Purpose strings: one `NS…UsageDescription` per permission actually used (Health share/update, Motion, Location When In Use), concrete purpose, localized; `PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy` kept in sync (UserDefaults CA92.1; Purchase History for RevenueCat, not linked, not tracking). Privacy copy never says "Data Not Collected" or "we don't collect your data": purchases go to RevenueCat under a random ID; nothing health-related leaves the phone; no ads, no tracking, no selling.
- Permissions are requested at the moment of need, never at launch: Health and notifications after the first workout (S16), location in Outdoor prep (S10b), motion when "Held to my chest" is chosen.
- Background modes only `audio` (spoken guidance) and `location` (outdoor route); entitlements only HealthKit. No other platform names in strings or metadata; no prices or invented numbers in copy; no medical claims (1.4.1).
- Review prompts only through `ReviewPromptPolicy`, never from a button (5.6.1).
- Steady program (plan `docs/plans/2026-10-08-steady-program.md`): promise "steadier on your feet / easier getting up from a chair" only; never "prevent falls", "fall risk", "build bone", "relieve pain" (`docs/design/steady-claims.md`, enforced by `copy_lint.py`). The 2-week self-check is self-counted, compared only with the user's own earlier checks done the same way (hands or not); no age norms, no risk labels, always "not a medical test". Program frame and self-check are free; earned reps (`RepLadder`) and support ladder are Pro.

## Chụp màn hình / test hooks
- Single capture hook: launch argument `-ScreenshotMode <state>` under `#if DEBUG`, seeding `iOS/App/Debug/Fixtures/en-US.json` and the named screen state (list in the plan's Localization section). Never add a second convention; revert other temporary hooks before a stage ends.
- Tests: Swift Testing; `@Suite(.serialized)` for suites sharing a store, UserDefaults or a `SKTestSession`; in-memory `ModelContainer` per test; purchases through `FakePurchaseBackend` (`StoreServiceTests`), `iOS/App/GentleWalk.storekit` only for `StoreConfigTests` and Xcode runs; dates, time zones and calendars injected.
