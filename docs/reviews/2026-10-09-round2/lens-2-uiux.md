# Round 2 · Lens 2 — UI/UX

_09/10/2026 · read-only review. IDs are numbered across the whole round-2 report._

## Verdict
**Fail** — no Critical, 14 Important (I6–I19) open. The screens changed since round 1 are tidy at default size and in dark mode, but the new stage recaps make Program and Today long, two paywall edge states can trap or loop her, and a code sweep of the rest of the app found defects round 1's screenshots could not show (iPad players, iPhone landscape, A+, dead buttons in Me, switch targets, dark-mode green text).
